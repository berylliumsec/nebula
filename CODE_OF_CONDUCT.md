# Nebula Code of Conduct

Nebula's community should be a respectful place to ask questions, contribute,
and learn. These expectations apply to everyone participating in this
repository, its issues, pull requests, Discussions, and other project-managed
community spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and respectful of people with different backgrounds,
  identities, abilities, experience levels, and perspectives.
- Give constructive feedback about the work. Explain disagreements without
  attacking people, and accept corrections in good faith.
- Respect other people's time, boundaries, privacy, and decisions to disengage.
- Keep discussions relevant and make room for newcomers to ask questions.
- Share only information and material you have permission to share. Use
  synthetic examples and authorized environments for security-related work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwanted sexual attention or advances
- Publishing or threatening to publish someone's private information without
  their permission
- Sustained disruption, targeted insults, or repeated unwanted contact
- Encouraging unauthorized access, sharing stolen data or credentials, or using
  community spaces to target people or systems without permission
- Retaliation against someone for raising a concern in good faith

## Report a concern

Email conduct reports privately to [info@berylliumsec.com](mailto:info@berylliumsec.com).

Do not post sensitive reports or personal information in public issues. A useful
private report identifies the relevant content or interaction, explains the
concern, and includes only the context needed to understand it. Do not gather
additional evidence in a way that puts you or anyone else at risk.

For abusive content on GitHub, you can also use
[GitHub's reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
For a vulnerability in Nebula itself, use [Security](SECURITY.md).

## Moderation

Project maintainers may remove or edit content, close or lock conversations,
issue a warning, or restrict participation in project-managed spaces when
behavior violates this code. The response should consider the conduct, its
impact, and any repeated behavior. A report involving a maintainer should be
reviewed by another maintainer where possible.

Reports should be shared only with people who need the information to review
the concern or address it. Avoid unnecessary disclosure of a reporter's identity
or private details. Concerns about a moderation decision can be raised through
the same private reporting contact.
